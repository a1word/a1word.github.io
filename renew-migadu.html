Renew Migadu?

17 November 2026

Last November, I signed up for the Migadu Micro email plan - $19/year:

Migadu Pricing

Basically, this was a 'test' account to use with my secondary domains, to ascertain if it suited my needs. These domains don't have a great volume of mail for either receiving or sending, and so that account worked out very well. 

When I paid the $19, there was an option to have my payment method put 'on file' to cover future renewals, but, as I prefer to have all card payments under my control, I chose not to.

I was aware that my subscription renewal date was soon, so looking in the Admin panel for how to pay, it only gives the option to prepay set amounts (in $ - 10/20/50/100/200) into the account balance.

So, if I were to renew in advance it looks like I would have to pay $20 to cover it, leaving a $1 balance in the account.

I assumed that, if I were to put a payment method on file I would be able to pay the exact $19 amount, but as I said earlier, I prefer not to leave my payment details on file.

I was in two minds whether or not to renew and I didn't like the idea of leaving funds in the account balance that I might never use.

So the day of renewal came and went . . .

The following day I received an email from Migadu:

We were sadly unable to renew your subscription.

Your account balance was insufficient and we could not charge your
default payment card. If not renewed within 7 days of subscription
expiry, the email service on the account will be suspended.

Please visit the billing center to renew manually at:

  https://admin.migadu.com/billing/subscription/renew

So then, I logged into my Admin account, and there was the option to renew at the price of $19.

I'm still undecided about renewing the subscription, but if I do, at least there is the option to pay the correct amount.

Note: This post isn't a review of the service - just a 'heads up' for anyone thinking of using it.
